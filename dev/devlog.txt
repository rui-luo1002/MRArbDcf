The results are not perfectly reproducable because:
1. finufft has different performance among versions, sometimes they change the grid oversamping ratio, which may dramatically affect the speed of this algorithm. Although I have fixed the oversampling ratio, I am not sure if they will change something other in the future.
2. different normalization method and value range calculation can make reconstruction metrics vary much, different phantom used can also make them vary much.
3. computation time itself is not reproducable per se.
